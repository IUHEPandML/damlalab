Who Are We

Data Analysis and Machine Learning Application laboratory (DAMLA-LAB) was built in 2022 within the concept of TUBİTAK 2232-A International Fellowship for Outstanding Researchers Project which was led by Dr. Hale Sert in the Department of Physics of Istanbul University. The laboratory is located in the building of D block G01 in the Faculty of Science, Department of Physics.

Our research groups, within the laboratory, continue their work in data analysis studies in particle physics as well as application of machine learning not only in particle physics but also in interdisciplinary areas.

Data analysis studies in particle physics involve the analysis of the data collected in the CMS (Compact Muon Solenoid) detector which is one of the LHC detectors as well as analysis of the data that are simulated for the experiments planned to be built in future. The Large Hadron Collider (LHC), located at CERN, in the border of France and Switzerland, which is one of the large particle physics research institutes in the world, accelerates the proton bunches to high energies and collides them in four different points. There are four different detectors placed in the collision points where each reserves different physics motivations. We are working on the CMS experiment. We focus especially on the tau lepton reconstruction and identification in both online (trigger) and offline levels as well as jet physics. Concerning the future experiments, simulation based studies for example for the FCC-hh are ongoing. Studies in these areas cover the Standard Model and Beyond the Standard Model Physics (New Physics) topics.

Machine learning application studies covers the classification of different signals and tagging different particles in high energy physics experiments as well as binary and multiclassification of medical images by using deep learning methods.
